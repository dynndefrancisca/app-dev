## Game of Thrones

**Game of Thrones** is an epic fantasy TV series based on *A Song of Ice and Fire* by **George R.R. Martin**.  
It follows the power struggles between noble families as they fight for control of the **Iron Throne** in the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros.

---

## My Favorite Character

### **Jon Snow**

> *"The North remembers."*  
> — Jon Snow

Jon Snow is my favorite character because he is **brave**, **honorable**, and always stands up for what is right.  
He starts as a member of the **Night’s Watch** and grows into one of the most respected leaders in the North.

**Key Traits:**
- Loyal  
- Selfless  
- Strong sense of duty  

---

## Favorite Location — *Dragonstone*

Dragonstone is an ancient island fortress with deep Targaryen roots.  
It’s surrounded by the **sea** and made from dark, **volcanic stone**.  
This location holds great historical and strategic importance throughout the series.


---

## Favorite Season — **Season 5**

Season 5 is my favorite because it features:
1. Jon Snow becoming **Lord Commander** of the Night’s Watch  
2. Daenerys ruling over Meereen  
3. The incredible **Battle of Hardhome** against the White Walkers

### Closing Thoughts

Game of Thrones isn’t just a fantasy story — it’s a tale of **power, loyalty, and survival**.  
Even though the series ended, its world and characters remain unforgettable.
